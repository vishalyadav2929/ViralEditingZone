Questions

Is it really free? Is there a watermark?

Yes, free, with no sign-up and no watermark. Export as many videos as you like.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Captions, editing and export all run inside your browser. Optional fast/AI features send only audio clips or caption text, never the video. See Privacy.

Does it understand Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes: Hindi in Devanagari, Hinglish in Roman letters, English and 30+ more. Short or noisy clips can have mistakes; every word can be fixed by hand.

Which phones and computers work?

Chrome or Edge on a computer is fastest. Android Chrome and iPhone Safari work too; use "Balanced" quality on phones.
