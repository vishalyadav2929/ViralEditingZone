How to add auto captions to a video — free, step by step

Updated 2026-11-02 · BrandBaazi

Captions make people stop scrolling: most short videos are watched with the sound off. Here is the fastest free way to add accurate, animated captions.

Try it now — free, no watermark, no login: Open BrandBaazi Studio →

Step 1 — Upload your video

Open BrandBaazi Studio and drop in your video. MP4, MOV (iPhone) and WebM all work. The file is read by your browser; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Step 2 — Choose the spoken language

Select the language spoken in the video. For Hindi or Hinglish speech on a computer, the Best for Hindi model is chosen automatically for higher accuracy. You can switch to Fast or Balanced under More options.

Want captions in a different script? Pick the caption language: Hindi (Devanagari), Hinglish (Roman Hindi) or English.

Step 3 — Let the captions generate

The first time, the speech model downloads once and is then cached. After that, captions appear with word-by-word timing, so each word can light up exactly when it is spoken.

Step 4 — Fix words and timing

  • Click any word on the timeline to edit it.
  • Use Split and merge to control how many words show at once.
  • Press 🧹 Remove umm/uh to clean filler words, and Cut silences to tighten the edit.

Step 5 — Pick a viral caption style

Choose from 90 animated styles — pop, bounce, karaoke highlight, bold outline, emoji and more. Change font, size, colour, outline and position in the Captions tab.

Step 6 — Export

Press Export video. You can download:

  • Video with captions burned in — original sound kept, no watermark.
  • Captions only (transparent) — a WebM with a real transparent background for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or After Effects.
  • Captions only (green screen) — for CapCut, InShot or VN: add it as an overlay and use Chroma Key.

FAQ

How do I add captions to a video automatically for free?

Upload the video to brandbaazi.in, choose the spoken language and the captions are generated automatically. Pick a style and export — free and without watermark.

Can I edit the auto-generated captions?

Yes. Click any word to change it, adjust timing, split or merge lines, and remove filler words like umm and uh.

Can I export only the captions to use in another editor?

Yes. Export captions only on a transparent background (WebM) or on a green screen (MP4) and place them over your video in any editor.
