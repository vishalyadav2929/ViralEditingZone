Why captions boost Reels and Shorts

  • Many people watch with the sound off — captions carry the message anyway.
  • Word-by-word highlights keep the eye moving, which helps watch time.
  • Captions make your content clear for viewers who speak a different language.

Where to place captions on a 9:16 video

Keep captions away from the bottom ~15% (likes, comments and the caption text sit there) and away from the right edge (action buttons). The middle third is the safest. In BrandBaazi, use the Vertical position slider in the Captions tab, or drag the caption on the preview.
