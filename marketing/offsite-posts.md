> 3. Pick a bold style (thick outline + one highlight colour reads best on phones) and keep captions
>    in the middle third of a 9:16 video, away from Instagram's buttons.
> 4. Export at 1080p. For YouTube, also download the SRT file and upload it in YouTube Studio.
