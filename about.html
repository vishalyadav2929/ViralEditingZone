FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no tokens or credits and no watermark. The site runs in your browser, so there's no per-video cost to pass on.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your video stays on your phone or computer. If Fast mode is on, only the audio is sent to our speech server to turn it into text, and you can switch Fast mode off under More options. See Privacy.

Will the export lose quality?

No. Exports keep your video's resolution, use a bitrate at least as high as the original, and copy the original sound unchanged.

Can I get Hinglish (Hindi in English letters)?

Yes. Choose Hinglish as the caption language, or switch any Hindi captions to Hinglish with one tap.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. You can also add it to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it like an app.

Can I use the captions in CapCut or InShot?

Yes. Export "transparent" or "green screen" captions and lay them over your video, or download an SRT file.
