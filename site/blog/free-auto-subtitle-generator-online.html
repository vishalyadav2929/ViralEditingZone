Subtitles vs captions — which do you need?

  • Burned-in captions are part of the video picture — best for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp, where you can't upload a subtitle file.
  • Subtitle files (SRT) are separate text with timings — best for YouTube, long videos, and editing software, because viewers can turn them on or off.

BrandBaazi gives you both from the same transcript.

Generate subtitles in 3 steps

  1. Open brandbaazi.in and upload your video — it stays on your device.
  2. Choose the spoken language (English, Hindi, Hinglish or 30+ others). Subtitles are generated with timing.
  3. Fix any word, then either Export video (burned in, no watermark) or press SRT to download the subtitle file.

Use the SRT file in other apps

  • YouTube: YouTube Studio → Subtitles → Add language → Upload file → "With timing" → choose the .srt.
  • Premiere Pro: File → Import the .srt, then drag it onto the timeline as a caption track.
  • DaVinci Resolve (free): import the .srt into the Media Pool and drag it onto the timeline — it becomes a subtitle track you can style.
