What makes caption typography go viral

  • Bold weight — thin fonts disappear on a phone.
  • Thick outline or shadow so text reads on any background.
  • 2–4 words at a time, with the spoken word highlighted.
  • One highlight colour (yellow, green or your brand colour).
  • UPPERCASE for hooks, normal case for long talking videos.

FAQ

What font do viral Reels captions use?

Mostly bold sans-serif fonts like Montserrat Extra Bold, Anton, Bebas Neue and Poppins, with a thick outline and one highlight colour.

What is the best font for subtitles?

A clean, bold sans-serif such as Montserrat or Poppins at a large size, with an outline or soft background box for contrast.

Which font is best for Hindi captions?

Noto Sans Devanagari for clean subtitles; Yatra One or Rozha One for bold, stylish Hindi captions.
